Features

Everything you need to
verify your documents

Built for designers, print shops, and anyone who needs to verify PDF dimensions before sending to print.

📐
Auto format detection
Instantly identifies A4, A3, Letter, Legal and custom sizes. Shows exact dimensions in mm and points.
⊞
Measurement grid
Overlay a precise grid in cm, mm, inches, or pixels. Toggle on/off anytime to check spacing and alignment.
📏
Ruler / measure tool
Click two points on your PDF to measure any distance. Results logged in a sidebar for reference.
🔄
Unit switching
Switch between cm, mm, inches, and pixels instantly. The grid and all measurements update in real time.
🔍
Zoom controls
Zoom in up to 4× for pixel-precise measurements on detailed documents and small labels.
🔒
100% private
Your PDF never leaves your device. Everything runs locally in your browser — no uploads, no servers.
